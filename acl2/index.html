      Supported math books
    
      Math book sources up to 2 MB (2,000,000 bytes) per file, with required shared Lisp utilities. Books needing oversized or unavailable dependencies are excluded. Sources download separately on first access. Compilation happens in your session.
      
    
      
    
      Restart for a fresh logical world, then certify, compile, and include the factorial book.
      
    
      (certify-book "books/arithmetic/factorial" 0 t)
(include-book "arithmetic/factorial" :dir :system :load-compiled-file :comp)
(factorial 10) ; 3628800
